02 — Hamiltonians

Four models. One quantum framework.

The repository implements multiple interacting many-body Hamiltonians to investigate different forms of quantum dynamics.

Ψ

Fermi–Hubbard Model

A fundamental model for studying interacting fermions, hopping dynamics and strong correlations in quantum many-body systems.

∿

Floquet Model

Periodically driven quantum dynamics used to explore non-equilibrium behavior and time-dependent evolution.

↻

Dzyaloshinskii–Moriya Interaction

An interaction producing non-trivial spin dynamics through antisymmetric exchange coupling.

Σ

Lipkin–Meshkov–Glick

A collective spin model useful for investigating long-range interactions and many-body quantum dynamics.

05 — Quantum Circuit

Digital quantum time evolution.

A simplified representation of the Qiskit workflow used to construct and evolve quantum circuits. 

from qiskit import QuantumCircuit

def time_evolution(n_qubits, steps):

    qc = QuantumCircuit(n_qubits)

    for step in range(steps):

        # Hamiltonian evolution
        for q in range(n_qubits):
            qc.h(q)

        # Trotterized interaction
        for q in range(n_qubits - 1):
            qc.cx(q, q + 1)

    return qc
